Chicken Karahi

Home

Description

Chicken Karahi is a traditional South Asian dish prepared with chicken, tomatoes, ginger, garlic and aromatic spices. It is usually cooked in a wok-like pan called a karahi.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 onions
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Green chillies
  • Red chilli powder
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a karahi.
  2. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and mix well.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and spices.
  5. Cook until the chicken becomes tender.
  6. Add green chillies and fresh coriander.
  7. Cook for a few more minutes.
  8. Serve hot with naan or rice.
