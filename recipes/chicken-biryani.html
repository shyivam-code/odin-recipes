Chicken Biryani

Home

Description

Chicken Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with aromatic spices, basmati rice and marinated chicken. It is known for its rich flavour and delicious aroma.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • Biryani masala
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander and mint

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  3. Fry onions until golden brown.
  4. Add chicken and cook until tender.
  5. Boil rice separately until partially cooked.
  6. Layer the rice and chicken in a cooking pot.
  7. Cook on low heat until fully done.
  8. Serve hot.
