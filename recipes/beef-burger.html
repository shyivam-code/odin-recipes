Beef Burger

Home

Description

A beef burger is a popular dish made with a seasoned beef patty placed inside a soft burger bun. It is commonly served with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese and sauces.

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef
  • 3 burger buns
  • 3 cheese slices
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Mayonnaise
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper.
  2. Shape the beef into burger patties.
  3. Heat oil in a pan.
  4. Cook the patties thoroughly on both sides.
  5. Add cheese slices while the patties are hot.
  6. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  7. Spread mayonnaise on the buns.
  8. Add lettuce, tomato, onion and beef patties.
  9. Close the burger and serve.
